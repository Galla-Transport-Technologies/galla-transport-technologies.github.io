# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Entity:** Galla Transport Technologies LLC

---

## 1. Scope & Purpose
Galla Transport Technologies LLC operates the **Tiger Transport** mobile platform and related fleet logistics systems. We are committed to protecting the privacy of student riders, school bus operators, and district administrative staff.

## 2. Information Collection and Handling
* **Operational Roster Data:** We only process student identification and roster parameters strictly required for onboard NFC badge logging, passenger manifest verification, and route reconciliation.
* **No Third-Party Monetization:** We do not sell, rent, lease, or distribute student, personnel, or district data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.
* **Educational Privacy Compliance:** System architecture and access controls are engineered in alignment with student data protection standards, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

## 3. Data Storage & Security
Roster and attendance logs are secured with role-based authentication, restricted district-level permissions, and encrypted transmission protocols.

## 4. Contact Us
For privacy questions, data requests, or compliance inquiries, please reach out through our [Contact Form](contact) or via official correspondence.
