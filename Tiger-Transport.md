# Tiger Transport

**Tiger Transport** is a specialized Android application developed by Galla Transport Technologies, custom-engineered for the **Macon R-1 School District** to streamline school bus operations, simplify driver workflows, and maximize student transit safety.

## Core Capabilities

* **Onboard NFC Tap Verification:** Rapid, hands-free student badge scanning eliminates paper roster tracking and minimizes driver distraction during loading and drop-offs.
* **Live Manifest & Route Tracking:** Real-time visibility into onboard student passenger counts, assigned run schedules (AM/PM), and route stops.
* **Manual Override & Search:** Instant manual student ID lookups and touch-keypad logging for students without physical badges or during route substitutions.
* **Offline-Resilient Architecture:** Local caching ensures uninterrupted scanning in cellular dead zones, automatically syncing records once connectivity resumes.
